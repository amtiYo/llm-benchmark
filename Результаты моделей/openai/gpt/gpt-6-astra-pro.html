Your field guide.

W A S D to wander. Move the mouse to look around.
Space to jump or swim up. Shift to sprint.
Ctrl to sneak — you won’t walk off ledges.

Hold left mouse to mine. The right tool is faster.
Right mouse to place a block or eat an apple.
Middle mouse picks the block you’re looking at.
1–9 / scroll selects a hotbar slot. Q drops one item.
Shift + Q drops a whole stack.

E opens your backpack and crafting recipes.
M opens your field map. H opens this guide.
F toggles fullscreen. P saves a picture.
F3 shows performance. Esc takes a break.

Explorer: mine, collect the bouncing drops, and craft. Deep falls and long dives can hurt. Apples restore health; rest heals you too. Return to camp anytime without losing items.

Architect: unlimited materials and no damage. Double-tap Space to toggle flight. Fly up with Space, down with Ctrl, faster with Shift. Pick any block from the field kit.
