HOW TO PLAY

🖱 Click world to lock mouse · ESC pause
WASD move · mouse look · Space jump / swim / fly-up
Shift sprint (double-tap W works too) · F toggle fly (Space/Shift up/down)
⛏ Hold Left-click: mine (cracks show progress) · Right-click: place
1–9 / wheel: select · Middle-click: pick targeted block
E inventory (free blocks!) · Q throw one block · F3 debug · H this help
📦 Broken blocks pop out as mini-cubes — walk over them to collect.
❤ Falling, drowning & the void hurt. Health regens slowly. Glowstone lights the night.
💾 World autosaves every 30 s. Find coal & iron ores deep underground!
